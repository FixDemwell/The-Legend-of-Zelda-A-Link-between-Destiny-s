# The-Legend-of-Zelda-A-Link-between-Destiny-s
Between Skyward Sword and Ocarina of Time, a reincarnated Link wakes after 100 years of sleep. Carry the Ocarina of Time across Hyrule, defeat the true Ganomisgan and deliver it to Zelda. Top-down, ~3-4 hours of gameplay, with NPCs, a shop and a boss fight. Unofficial fan game, not affiliated with Nintendo.
